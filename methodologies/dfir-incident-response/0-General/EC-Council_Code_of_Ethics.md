The **EC-Council Code of Ethics** establishes mandatory standards of professional conduct that all candidates and certified professionals (including CHFI credential holders) must strictly uphold. Violations can lead to review panel investigation, decertification, or legal action. 

The key rules and guidelines are structured across several core ethical areas:

### 1. Privacy & Confidentiality
* **Client Data Protection**: Certified members must protect private and confidential information gained during professional work.
* **No Unauthorized Transfer**: Members must not collect, sell, give, or transfer any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or Social Security numbers) to a third party without explicit client consent.

### 2. Intellectual Property & Innovation
* **Respecting IP Rights**: Professionals must respect and protect the intellectual property of others by relying on their own innovation and efforts.
* **Reporting Dangers**: Members are obligated to disclose and report potential security risks or dangers to e-commerce clients, the Internet community, or the public.

### 3. Competence & Professional Care
* **Scope of Competence**: Services must be provided strictly within areas of true individual competence, with honest disclosure of experience and education limits.
* **Project Management & Care**: Members must maintain ethical conduct, professional care, and proper management on all professional assignments without prejudice.

### 4. Legal Integrity & Financial Honesty
* **Lawful Software and Tools**: Members must never knowingly use software, utilities, or processes obtained or retained illegally or unethically.
* **No Deceptive Financials**: Engaging in deceptive financial practices such as bribery, double-billing, or improper invoicing is strictly prohibited.
* **Property Protection**: Client and employer property must be accessed and used strictly with authorization and consent.

### 5. Conflict of Interest & Exam Integrity
* **Disclosure Requirements**: Members must disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest to clients, employers, or EC-Council.
* **Item Writing Rule**: Anyone who participates in **Item Writing** for any EC-Council certification examination is strictly prohibited from sitting for that same exam.
* **Exam Confidentiality**: Members must not participate in cheating incidents, exam leaks, brain dumps, or any misconduct that compromises exam integrity or confidentiality.

### 6. Strict Prohibition of Malicious & Black-Hat Activities
* **No Malicious Associations**: Certified professionals must not associate with malicious hackers or participate in malicious activities.
* **No Black-Hat Communities**: Joining, preaching in, or associating with underground black-hat hacking communities is grounds for immediate credential revocation.
* **Authorized Engagements Only**: All security testing, penetration testing, and forensic investigations must be explicitly authorized and conducted within strict legal boundaries.

### 7. Certification, Logo & Trademark Compliance
* **Accurate Representation**: Members must not make misleading or unauthorized claims regarding their certification scope or bring EC-Council into disrepute.
* **Compliance with Logo Guidelines**: Misleading use of certificates, titles, marks, or logos is prohibited, and all trademark usage must immediately cease if certification is suspended or revoked.
